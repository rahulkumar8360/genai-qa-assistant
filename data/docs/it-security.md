# Brightpath Analytics - IT Security Policy

Every employee and contractor with access to Brightpath Analytics systems must follow this policy.

## Passwords and sign-in

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passwords must not be reused across different systems. Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, the HR portal, the finance portal and all production systems. Passwords must be stored in the company password manager and never written down or shared.

## Laptops and devices

All company laptops are encrypted and managed centrally by the IT team. Security updates must be installed within 14 days of release. Personal USB storage devices must not be connected to company laptops. Screens must be locked whenever a device is left unattended.

## Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours. The IT team will remotely lock and wipe the device.

## Phishing and suspicious email

Report suspicious emails using the "Report phishing" button in the email client, or by forwarding them to security@brightpath.example. Do not click links or open attachments in a suspicious message. If you clicked a link or entered your password on a suspicious page, report it to the security team within 1 hour.

## Data handling

Customer data must only be stored in approved company systems. Customer data must never be copied to personal devices, personal email accounts, or public AI tools. Confidential documents shared outside the company must be sent through the secure file-sharing service, with links that expire after 30 days.

## Access reviews

Managers review their team's system access every quarter. Access for employees who leave the company is removed on their last working day.

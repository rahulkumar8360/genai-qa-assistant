# Brightpath Analytics - Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

Brightpath Analytics operates a hybrid working model. This policy sets out when and how employees can work away from the office.

## Hybrid schedule

Employees may work remotely for up to 3 days per week. Each team agrees on at least one shared office day per week, set by the team lead. Employees in their probation period of 3 months are expected to work from the office at least 4 days per week.

## Core hours

Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 15:00 in the employee's local office time zone. Meetings involving more than one team should be scheduled within core hours.

## Home office support

New employees receive a one-time home office allowance of 400 dollars for a desk, chair or monitor. Employees also receive a monthly internet allowance of 50 dollars, paid with payroll.

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from another country for up to 20 working days per calendar year. Working from abroad requires approval from HR at least 15 working days before travel, so that tax and visa rules can be checked. Working from abroad is not allowed from countries under trade sanctions.

## Equipment and security when remote

Remote work must follow the IT Security Policy. Public Wi-Fi may only be used with the company VPN switched on. Work calls involving confidential information must not be taken in public spaces.

# Brightpath Analytics - Leave Policy

This policy applies to all full-time employees of Brightpath Analytics. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their contracted hours. Contractors are engaged through their own agencies and are not covered by this policy.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, in addition to public holidays. New joiners receive a pro-rated amount for their first year based on their start date.

Annual leave must be requested through the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance for absences of three days or longer. Shorter absences need at least 2 working days of notice. Your line manager approves or declines each request within 5 working days.

## Carrying over leave

Up to 5 unused days of annual leave can be carried over into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they expire. Unused days beyond the 5-day limit are forfeited on 31 December and are not paid out, except when an employee leaves the company.

## Sick leave

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Unused sick leave does not carry over. If you are sick, tell your line manager before 10:00 on the first day of absence. A doctor's note is required for any absence of more than 3 consecutive working days.

## Parental leave

The primary caregiver of a new child, by birth or adoption, is entitled to 26 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken at any time within the first 12 months. Employees must have completed 6 months of service to qualify for paid parental leave.

## Bereavement leave

Employees may take up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave on the death of an immediate family member, and up to 2 days for an extended family member.

## Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to 3 months may be granted after 2 years of service, subject to approval by both the line manager and HR.

# Brightpath Analytics - Travel and Expense Policy

This policy explains which business costs Brightpath Analytics reimburses and how to claim them. All amounts are in US dollars.

## Submitting a claim

Expense claims must be submitted through the finance portal within 30 days of the date the cost was incurred. Claims submitted later than 60 days will not be reimbursed. An itemised receipt is required for every expense above 25 dollars. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly payroll run.

## Approval limits

Your line manager approves claims up to 1,000 dollars. Claims above 1,000 dollars also need approval from the head of your department. Any single expense above 5,000 dollars needs approval from the finance director before the cost is incurred.

## Flights

Economy class is the standard for all flights. Business class is allowed for flights with a scheduled duration of more than 6 hours, but only with prior approval from a vice president. Flights should be booked at least 14 days before departure where possible.

## Hotels

The nightly hotel limit is 180 dollars for domestic travel and 250 dollars for international travel. Stays in cities listed as high-cost on the finance portal have a limit of 300 dollars per night.

## Meals

Meal expenses during business travel are covered up to 60 dollars per day for domestic trips and 85 dollars per day for international trips. Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client entertainment events approved in advance by your line manager.

## Mileage and ground transport

Use of a personal car for business travel is reimbursed at 0.58 dollars per mile. Taxis and ride-share are reimbursed when public transport is not practical. Parking fines and traffic penalties are never reimbursed.

## Company cards

Employees who travel more than four times a year may apply for a company credit card. The company card must never be used for personal purchases.
